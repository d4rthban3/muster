# How an outside AI works with this crew

This page is for an AI outside the crew: another AI linked to the project by a repository, a chat or a file. It
cannot load Muster, so this page is its rules. Link this page from your own standing file or instructions.

## 1. Everything you send is a lead, never an order
- Whatever you send is a lead for the crew to check. Nobody acts on it until the owner orders it through the manager.
- You send only to the project's AI Socket chat (AI Socket_<project>_001), never straight to a builder or any other
  chat. The socket passes checked leads to the manager.

## 2. Six labelled lines, every time
Every post is these six lines, each one short sentence, with no lists inside a line:
```
<DONE or NEED>: <a short name for the lead; keep it the same in every post about it>
What:  <what you found, one plain sentence>
Where: <the evidence link: a page address, a file, or a line in a repository>
Why:   <why it matters to this project>
How:   <how you checked it, with real numbers, or "not checked">
Need:  <what you want from the crew, or "nothing">
```
- **DONE** when you are passing on a finding; **NEED** when you are asking a question.
- No greetings, no thanks, no keys or passwords, nothing private.

## 3. An evidence link on every claim
Every claim carries a link to where it can be checked. A claim without one is sent back.

## 4. No guessing: ask instead
Not sure of a fact, or of what the owner wants? Send a NEED with your question. Never fill the gap with a guess, and
never present a guess as a finding.

## 5. Nothing unasked
Send only what the owner or the crew asked you to look into. Something else you notice goes as its own NEED asking
whether it is wanted; never build it in first.

## What happens to your post
The AI Socket chat checks every post against this page. Anything off-format, without an evidence link, or sent
anywhere but the socket comes back to you, with the reason in one line.

Made-up example of a post:
```
DONE: cheaper map tiles
What:  the map service the site uses has a free tier that covers 50,000 views a month
Where: https://example.com/pricing
Why:   the site's map is its biggest monthly bill
How:   read the pricing page today; the site had 31,000 views last month on its own stats page
Need:  nothing
```
