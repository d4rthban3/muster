# The crew

Kept by the manager. One row per chat that is running now. Old chats move to "Archived" (never deleted).

## The jobs
| Worker | Its one job | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Manager | the owner's go-between and record keeper: sorts what the owner says, writes orders, keeps the owner's list and each worker's rules page, closes items against the owner's words | builds, passes or fails anything |
| Builder (A, B, C...) | builds what its order says, in its own copy of the project folder | passes its own work |
| Designer | builds what people see (pages, looks, pictures) | changes what is behind the scenes |
| Inspector | checks builders' work behind the scenes, and the manager's paperwork | builds, fixes or writes files |
| Design_Inspector | checks the designer's work and anything visitors see (added when three or more workers build) | builds, fixes or writes files |
| Sketchpad (add-on) | a place to play with ideas | turns an idea into work; only the owner does, through the manager |
| AI_Socket (add-on) | brings in work from other AIs; it arrives as a lead to check, never an order | gives another AI keys or private files |
| Agent_Teacher (add-on) | "Train your own agent in Claude Console": the agent learns from its saved memory, its checked results and reviewed changes to its instructions, not by retraining the model | spends money or uses a key without the owner's yes |
