# Risks

Kept by the manager. The one page for open risks: nothing that might go wrong lives as a loose line on the board or in a note. A risk is open until what closes it has happened; then it moves to "Closed" with the date.

One entry per risk:
- **Risk:** <short name>
  - **Owner:** <the worker or the owner who answers for it>
  - **What would go wrong:** <one plain sentence>
  - **What closes it:** <the check, order or answer that ends the risk>
