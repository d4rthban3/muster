# The five-line starting note

The manager gives every new chat these five lines, nothing more. A worker's note opens, right under its first line, with
the quiet-window line, the re-read line and the one-app line below; the manager's own note leaves it out. The chat then reads its own rules page (in
`muster/rules/`), not the whole decisions log.

```
Your five-line starting note, from the manager (or from the setup, for the first crew):
Your end-of-turn reply is a short card for the owner to read in your own window (title, two or three bullets); the full report still goes by message.
Re-read your rules page before starting each new order.
Computer use only on the manager's order naming the owner's words and the one app; never the whole screen or the clipboard; if a step needs more, send STUCK.
Job:       <the worker's job in one line>
Owns:      <its folders or files>
Not yours: <what it must not touch; anything it is not sure is its own, it asks>
Open:      <items already waiting for it, by their exact job names>
First:     <read your worker definition and follow it; then your rules page, your last handover, and the first job by name>
```
