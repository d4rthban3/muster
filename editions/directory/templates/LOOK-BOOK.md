# Look book

Kept by the manager. One line for each look the owner has approved, with a picture link. The designer builds to
these looks and the design inspector checks against them; a look not listed here is the owner's call, never a guess.

Format: `<date> | <what it is> | <the owner's words approving it> | <picture link>`

Made-up example:
`2026-01-31 | home page, light and dark | "yes, that one" (item 4) | screenshots/home-light-dark.png`
