# The owner's list

One file, always this one, updated in place. Open items at the top, ideas in their own section, closed items at the
bottom. The manager keeps it; the owner never has to tick anything. To bring a closed item back, say "reopen" and its
number.

Each item has five parts:
1. **The owner's words**, exactly as said, never reworded.
2. **Kind**: order, inquiry or idea (the manager's sort, shown here, not asked).
3. **Did**: what the manager did with it. For an inquiry: what it looked at and found. For an order: which worker has it.
4. **Stands**: with a worker, waiting on you, or done.
5. **Checked**: once done, how the result was checked against the owner's words (not only against the order).

Made-up example:
```
7. "the blueprint is a mess, clean it up"
   Kind: inquiry
   Did: looked at the blueprint page: 62 boxes and 99 wires on one screen, hard to follow. Asked: group them, or hide the old ones?
   Stands: waiting on you
   Checked: (not done yet)
```
